About CMK

CMK Music Production

At CMK Music Production, we believe music is more than sound — it’s a story, a heartbeat, and a bridge between people. Founded by passionate producers and sound engineers, CMK is built on the vision of elevating African and global sound through creativity, precision, and authenticity.

From intimate studio sessions to large-scale productions, CMK specializes in music production, recording, mixing & mastering, live sound design, and artist development. Our focus is to turn every concept into a sonic experience that speaks to the soul and resonates with audiences worldwide.

Our team has worked with emerging and established artists, crafting signature sounds across genres — from hip-hop, R&B, and gospel, to amapiano and Afro-fusion. Whether you’re a solo artist looking to record your next hit, or a brand in need of cinematic sound for your next campaign, CMK is your creative partner in sound.
